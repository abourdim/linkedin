4. Advanced Features

Keyboard Shortcuts

ShortcutAction
Ctrl + KOpen command palette (quick search)
EscClose palette / mobile sidebar

Themes

7 themes — click the colored dots at the bottom of the sidebar: Light, Dark, Blue, Contrast, Golden Age, Alhambra, Medina

Edit Mode

Click the pencil button in the toolbar to enable edit mode. Edit content directly — changes are saved locally (localStorage).

Flash Cards

In "Interview Prep > Flash Cards" — 30 interactive flip cards:

  • TECH (blue) — 10 technical skill cards
  • Q&A (orange) — 10 interview question cards
  • VOCAB (green) — 10 FR/EN vocabulary cards

Copy Prompts & Queries

  • Prompts: click Copy button on each prompt box
  • Queries: click directly on the query text (turns green = copied)

6. Google Analytics

1 Go to analytics.google.com
2 Select property "Portfolio Abdelhak"
3 Real-time: see who is online now
4 Reports > Engagement > Pages: most viewed pages
5 Reports > Acquisition: where visitors come from
Recruiter tip: After sending your portfolio link to a recruiter, check the Real-time report to see if they opened it.

7. Offline (PWA)

The site works offline thanks to the service worker. After a first visit, pages are cached automatically.
