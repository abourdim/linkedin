================================================================================
        2-MINUTE PITCH — JOB SEARCH IN LUXEMBOURG
        "Tell me about yourself"
        Abdelhak Bourdim — Senior Embedded Software Engineer
================================================================================

TIMING: ~2 minutes (280 words) — dynamic, professional pace
CONTEXT: interview for an embedded position in Luxembourg (cross-border worker from Lorraine)


================================================================================
SCRIPT (learn it, don't read it)
================================================================================

Hello, I'm Abdelhak Bourdim, a senior embedded software engineer
with over 20 years of experience.

                        [PAUSE — 5 sec]

My core expertise is developing low-level embedded software —
firmware, drivers, communication stacks — in C/C++ on ARM Cortex,
STM32, Microchip and NXP architectures, from bare metal to RTOS
such as FreeRTOS and VxWorks, and embedded Linux.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

What makes my background distinctive is the diversity of
safety-critical sectors I've worked in:

In aerospace, at Safran, Thales and Zodiac, I developed low-level
drivers and application layers compliant with DO-178 and MISRA C —
over 6 years in this sector.

In medical devices, at GE Healthcare and Sorin, I integrated
CANOpen stacks and developed BLE modules for pacemakers,
compliant with IEC 62304.

In IoT cybersecurity, at Enedis, I led security audits and
penetration testing on Linky smart meter concentrators.

And most recently, in EV charging at E2-CAD, I work on
ISO 15118 integration, the V2G protocol and commissioning
of charging stations.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

Beyond the technical side, I founded Workshop-DIY, a nonprofit
offering robotics and electronics workshops for young people.
It reflects my passion for knowledge sharing and innovation.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

What attracts me to Luxembourg is its unique ecosystem that brings
together satellites, automotive, industry and fintech in an
international and multicultural environment. My trilingual profile
— French, English and Arabic — and my cross-sector experience
fit naturally into this dynamic.

I'm based in Lorraine, ready to work as a cross-border commuter,
and available immediately.

I'd be happy to go into more detail about any of my projects.


================================================================================
PITCH STRUCTURE (cheat sheet)
================================================================================

  1. WHO I AM              -> Name + title + 20 years          [10 sec]
  2. WHAT I DO             -> C/C++, RTOS, Linux, MCU          [15 sec]
  3. MY PROOF (4 sectors):                                      [60 sec]
     * Aerospace  -> Safran, Thales, Zodiac (DO-178, MISRA C)
     * Medical    -> GE Healthcare, Sorin (IEC 62304)
     * Cyber      -> Enedis, Linky (pentest, audit)
     * EV         -> E2-CAD (ISO 15118, OCPP)
  4. WHAT SETS ME APART    -> Workshop-DIY, passion             [15 sec]
  5. WHY LUXEMBOURG        -> Ecosystem, multicultural          [15 sec]
  6. CLOSE                 -> Cross-border, available            [5 sec]

                                            TOTAL = 2 min


================================================================================
DELIVERY TIPS
================================================================================

* Speak at a natural pace — not too fast, not too slow
* Maintain eye contact (or look at the camera in video calls)
* Confident and direct tone — highlight your track record without arrogance
* PAUSES are essential — they show mastery and confidence
* Do NOT recite — know the blocks, not the text word for word
* If the interviewer reacts, adapt and engage in dialogue
* Have 3 anecdotes ready to elaborate on each sector
* In Luxembourg, emphasize multilingualism and international openness
* Mention cross-border worker status naturally, without overdoing it
