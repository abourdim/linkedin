Script

Hello, I'm Abdelhak Bourdim, a senior embedded software engineer with over 20 years of experience.

— PAUSE —

My core expertise is developing low-level embedded software — firmware, drivers, communication stacks — in C/C++ on ARM Cortex, STM32, Microchip and NXP architectures, from bare metal to RTOS such as FreeRTOS and VxWorks, and embedded Linux.

— PAUSE —

What makes my background distinctive is the diversity of safety-critical sectors I've worked in:

In aerospace, at Safran, Thales and Zodiac, I developed low-level drivers and application layers compliant with DO-178 and MISRA C — over 6 years in this sector.

In medical devices, at GE Healthcare and Sorin, I integrated CANOpen stacks and developed BLE modules for pacemakers, compliant with IEC 62304.

In IoT cybersecurity, at Enedis, I led security audits and penetration testing on Linky smart meter concentrators.

And most recently, in EV charging at E2-CAD, I work on ISO 15118 integration, the V2G protocol and commissioning of charging stations.

— PAUSE —

Beyond the technical side, I founded Workshop-DIY, a nonprofit offering robotics and electronics workshops for young people. It reflects my passion for knowledge sharing and innovation.

— PAUSE —

What attracts me to Luxembourg is its unique ecosystem that brings together satellites, automotive, industry and fintech in an international and multicultural environment. My trilingual profile — French, English and Arabic — and my cross-sector experience fit naturally into this dynamic.

I'm based in Lorraine, ready to work as a cross-border commuter, and available immediately.

I'd be happy to go into more detail about any of my projects.

Delivery Tips

  • Speak at a natural pace — not too fast, not too slow
  • Maintain eye contact (or look at the camera in video calls)
  • Confident and direct tone — highlight your track record without arrogance
  • PAUSES are essential — they show mastery and confidence
  • Do NOT recite — know the blocks, not the text word for word
  • If the interviewer reacts, adapt and engage in dialogue
  • Have 3 anecdotes ready to elaborate on each sector
  • In Luxembourg, emphasize multilingualism and international openness
  • Mention cross-border worker status naturally, without overdoing it
