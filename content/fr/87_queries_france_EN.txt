PART 8 — PRACTICAL TIPS (FRANCE-SPECIFIC)
================================================================================

1. FREQUENCY: check LinkedIn 2x per day (morning + evening)
2. ACT FAST: apply within 48h (high competition in France)
3. USE BOTH LANGUAGES: search in French AND English
4. COMBINE PLATFORMS:
   - LinkedIn + APEC (executives/engineers) + France Travail (ex Pole Emploi)
   - Welcome to the Jungle (startups/tech)
   - Indeed France + Monster France
5. CUSTOMIZE: tailor each application (cover letter often required in France,
   collective bargaining agreements Syntec/Metallurgy)
6. ACTIVATE "Open to Work": visible to recruiters only
   - Titles: Embedded Software Engineer, Firmware Engineer,
     Ingenieur Logiciel Embarque
   - Location: France, Paris, Ile-de-France, Toulouse, Grenoble, Lyon
   - Type: Full-time / CDI
   - Start: Immediately
7. FOLLOW COMPANIES: follow target company pages
   (Safran, Thales, Valeo, Stellantis, Schneider Electric, Alten, Akkodis)
   to see their posts and job openings early
8. PUBLISH CONTENT: share technical embedded articles
   to increase your visibility with French recruiters
9. FRANCE-SPECIFIC:
   - Check collective bargaining agreements (Syntec for consulting firms,
     Metallurgy for industry) to understand salary grids
   - Consulting firms (Alten, Akkodis, Assystem) hire heavily in embedded
   - Toulouse = aerospace hub, Grenoble = semiconductors/IoT hub,
     Paris/IDF = all industries, Sophia Antipolis = telecom/IoT
   - "Cadre" status is standard for engineers in France
