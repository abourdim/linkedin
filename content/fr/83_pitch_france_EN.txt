        "Tell me about yourself"
        Abdelhak Bourdim — Senior Embedded Software Engineer

CONTEXT: interview for an embedded role in France (Paris / Ile-de-France)


================================================================================
SCRIPT (memorize the blocks, don't read)
================================================================================

Hello, I'm Abdelhak Bourdim, a senior embedded software engineer
with over 20 years of experience.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

My core expertise is developing low-level embedded software —
firmware, drivers, communication stacks — in C/C++ on ARM Cortex,
STM32, Microchip, and NXP architectures, from bare metal to RTOS
like FreeRTOS and VxWorks, and Embedded Linux.

                        [PAUSE — 2 sec]

What sets my career apart is the range of critical industries
I've worked in:

In aerospace, at Safran and Thales, I developed low-level drivers
and application layers compliant with DO-178.

In medical devices, at GE Healthcare and Sorin, I integrated
CANOpen stacks and developed BLE modules for a pacemaker,
compliant with IEC 62304.

In IoT cybersecurity, at Enedis, I led security audits and
penetration tests on Linky smart meter concentrators.

And most recently, in EV charging at E2-CAD, I work on ISO 15118
integration, V2G protocols, and charging station commissioning.

                        [PAUSE — 2 sec]

Beyond the technical side, I founded Workshop-DIY, a non-profit
that delivers robotics and electronics workshops for young people.
It reflects my passion for sharing knowledge, innovation, and
taking initiative.

                        [PAUSE — 2 sec]

What interests me about your company is [ADAPT based on company
— see variants below]. My multi-sector experience allows me to
integrate quickly and bring a cross-functional perspective.

I'm based in the Paris / Ile-de-France area and immediately
available.

I'd be happy to go into more detail about any of my projects.

================================================================================
DELIVERY TIPS (FRENCH MARKET)
================================================================================

• Natural, dynamic pace — more fluid than Swiss style, but stay composed
• Maintain eye contact (or look at camera in video calls)
• Show enthusiasm — this is valued in France
• PAUSES remain important — they structure the narrative
• Do NOT recite — know the blocks, not word for word
• If the interviewer reacts, adapt and engage in dialogue
• Have 3 anecdotes ready to go deeper on each sector
• Do NOT mention salary expectations in the pitch
• Emphasize teamwork and company culture
