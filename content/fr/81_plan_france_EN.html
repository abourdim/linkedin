Part 2 — Status and Working Conditions

Your Status FRENCH CITIZEN

  • French nationality — no work permit needed
  • You can work immediately with no administrative procedures
  • CDI (permanent contract) is the preferred contract type
  • Trial period: 4 months (executive), renewable once (8 months max)

Syntec Collective Agreement (ESN)

Applicable to Most ESN in France
  • Classification: position 3.1 or 3.2 for a senior profile
  • Paid leave: 25 working days + RTT (company-dependent, 8-12 days/year)
  • Mandatory company health insurance (mutuelle)
  • Profit-sharing (participation / interessement) depending on company

Benefits to Negotiate

  • Remote work: 2-3 days/week has become the norm post-COVID
  • Holiday bonus (Syntec convention)
  • Company savings plan (PEE) / PERCO
  • Meal vouchers (tickets restaurant)
  • Continuing education (CPF)
  • Company car (for some positions)

Part 3 — Adapting Your CV to the French Market

French CV Format

  1. NO PHOTO (anti-discrimination law, May 27, 2008)
  2. NO DATE OF BIRTH or age
  3. NO NATIONALITY or marital status
  4. SKILLS SECTION at the top of the CV (clearly visible)
  5. FORMAT: 2 pages max, PDF, reverse chronological
  6. CLEAR TITLE: "Senior Embedded Software Engineer — 20+ years experience"
  7. SUMMARY: 3-4 lines summarizing profile and objective
  8. LANGUAGE: French for positions in France, English for multinationals
  9. REFERENCES: "Available upon request" is acceptable
  10. KEYWORDS: adapt for ATS (automated tracking systems)

Your Specific Strengths for France

  • 20+ years in critical embedded systems = highly sought-after senior profile
  • Experience at Safran, Thales, Valeo = references recognized by all recruiters
  • Standards DO-178, IEC 62304, MISRA C = guarantee of rigor and quality
  • Embedded cybersecurity (Enedis/Linky) = rare skill in high demand
  • Trilingual French/English/Arabic = asset for international projects
  • Founder of Workshop-DIY = valued community engagement

CV Actions

  • Remove photo, date of birth, nationality from CV
  • Add a "Technical Skills" section at the top of the CV
  • Add a 3-4 line summary/objective
  • Adapt keywords for ATS (automated tracking systems)
  • Prepare a French version and an English version
  • Write a personalized cover letter for each target company

Part 5 — Week-by-Week Action Plan

Week 1-2: Preparation
  • Update CV to French format (no photo, no personal info)
  • Add "Technical Skills" section + summary
  • Prepare 2 versions: FR + EN (for multinationals)
  • Update LinkedIn profile
  • Add "Open to work" on LinkedIn, target France
  • Write a customizable cover letter template
  • Prepare a short version of the pitch (2 minutes)
Week 2-3: Register on Job Boards
  • linkedin.com — set up alerts for "embarque" + "embedded" + target cities
  • indeed.fr — general, widely used in France
  • apec.fr — specific to executives (your profile)
  • welcometothejungle.com — startups and tech companies
  • frenchweb.fr/jobs — tech/digital specialist
  • pole-emploi.fr — national portal
  • monster.fr — general
Week 2-4: Spontaneous Applications
  • Identify 15-20 target companies (see Part 4 list)
  • Send spontaneous applications by email to HR + technical managers
  • Customize each cover letter for the company
  • Priority targets: Safran, Thales, Valeo, Capgemini Engineering, Schneider Electric
  • Apply on career websites of target companies
Week 3-5: Networking
  • Connect on LinkedIn with embedded engineers in France
  • Join LinkedIn groups: "Embedded France", "Systemes Embarques", "French Tech"
  • Contact specialized embedded recruiters: Hays, Michael Page, Robert Half, Expectra, Randstad Technologies
  • Attend tech meetups (meetup.com — Paris, Lyon, Toulouse)
  • If possible, visit "Embedded World" or "Enova Paris" trade shows
Week 4-6: Interviews
  • Prepare for technical interviews (C/C++, RTOS, protocols, architecture)
  • Prepare questions about the Syntec agreement and benefits
  • Know market salaries (don't undervalue yourself)
  • Negotiate: salary, remote work, RTT, training, meal vouchers
Week 6-8: Finalization
  • Sign the contract (CDI preferred)
  • Negotiate start date (notice period if currently employed)
  • Organize relocation if changing region
  • Update health insurance and administrative documents
