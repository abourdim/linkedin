Part 4 — Your Strengths for the Gulf

  1. Native Arabic — HUGE advantage in the Gulf
  2. 20+ years in critical embedded systems (aerospace, medical)
  3. Defense experience (Safran, Thales) — booming sector
  4. Embedded cybersecurity — highly sought-after
  5. Certified standards: DO-178, IEC 62304, MISRA C

Part 5 — Mistakes to Avoid

  • Do not neglect Arabic in your CV and interviews
  • Do not underestimate packages (negotiate housing, transport, flights)
  • Do not ignore local culture (respect, punctuality, dress code)
  • Do not send a generic CV (adapt to the country and sector)
  • Do not ignore local recruiters (they know the market)
