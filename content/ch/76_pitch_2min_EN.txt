================================================================================
        2-MINUTE PITCH — JOB INTERVIEW
        "Tell me about yourself"
        Abdelhak Bourdim — Senior Embedded Software Engineer
================================================================================

TIMING: ~2 minutes (280 words) — calm, professional pace
CONTEXT: interview for an embedded role in Western Switzerland


================================================================================
SCRIPT (memorize the blocks, don't read)
================================================================================

Hello, I'm Abdelhak Bourdim, a senior embedded software engineer
with over 20 years of experience.

                        [PAUSE — 5 sec]

My core expertise is developing low-level embedded software —
firmware, drivers, communication stacks — in C/C++ on ARM Cortex,
STM32, Microchip, and NXP architectures, from bare metal to RTOS
like FreeRTOS and VxWorks, and Embedded Linux.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

What sets my career apart is the range of critical industries
I've worked in:

In aerospace, at Safran and Thales, I developed low-level drivers
and application layers compliant with DO-178.

In medical devices, at GE Healthcare and Sorin, I integrated
CANOpen stacks and developed BLE modules for a pacemaker,
compliant with IEC 62304.

In IoT cybersecurity, at Enedis, I led security audits and
penetration tests on Linky smart meter concentrators.

And most recently, in EV charging at E2-CAD, I work on ISO 15118
integration, V2G protocols, and charging station commissioning.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

Beyond the technical side, I founded Workshop-DIY, a non-profit
that delivers robotics workshops for young people. It reflects
my passion for sharing knowledge and innovation.

                        [PAUSE — 3 sec]

Today, I want to bring this multi-sector experience to
Western Switzerland, where the quality standards and rigor
match the way I work.

I'm a French national, eligible for the G cross-border permit,
and available to start quickly.

I'd be happy to go into more detail about any of my projects.


================================================================================
PITCH STRUCTURE (cheat sheet)
================================================================================

  1. WHO I AM             → Name + title + 20 yrs      [10 sec]
  2. WHAT I DO            → C/C++, RTOS, Linux, MCU    [15 sec]
  3. MY PROOF (4 sectors):                              [60 sec]
     • Aero    → Safran, Thales (DO-178)
     • Medical → GE Healthcare, Sorin (IEC 62304)
     • Cyber   → Enedis, Linky (pentesting)
     • V2G     → E2-CAD (ISO 15118, OCPP)
  4. WHAT SETS ME APART   → Workshop-DIY, passion       [15 sec]
  5. WHY SWITZERLAND      → Quality, rigor, G permit    [15 sec]
  6. CLOSE                → Available, eager             [5 sec]

                                            TOTAL ≈ 2 min


================================================================================
DELIVERY TIPS
================================================================================

• Speak SLOWLY — Swiss pace is calmer than French pace
• Maintain eye contact (or look at camera in video calls)
• Factual and modest tone — no boasting
• PAUSES are key — they show confidence
• Do NOT recite — know the blocks, not word for word
• If the interviewer reacts, adapt and engage in dialogue
• Have 3 anecdotes ready to go deeper on each sector


================================================================================
COMPANY-SPECIFIC VARIANTS
================================================================================

IF MEDICAL COMPANY (Medtronic, Stryker, etc.):
  → Emphasize GE Healthcare + Sorin + PK Vitality
  → Mention IEC 62304, Doxygen, in vitro testing
  → "Medical development demands rigorous documentation
     and traceability — something I've practiced for years."

IF CYBERSECURITY COMPANY (Kudelski, WISeKey, etc.):
  → Emphasize Enedis/Linky, pentesting, code audits
  → Mention Kali Linux, Nmap, Scapy, SDR, HackRF
  → "Auditing Linky concentrators gave me a comprehensive
     view of embedded system vulnerabilities."

IF AEROSPACE COMPANY (RUAG, Beyond Gravity, etc.):
  → Emphasize Safran + Thales + Zodiac = 6+ years in aero
  → Mention DO-178, MISRA C, Lauterbach TRACE32
  → "I spent 6 years in aerospace, from Zodiac to Safran,
     working on SSPC systems and defense radios."

IF WATCHMAKING/PRECISION (Rolex, CSEM, etc.):
  → Emphasize precision, microcontrollers, low-level work
  → Mention dsPIC, NXP LPC, SPI/I2C drivers, bare metal
  → "For 8 years at Cartadis, I built complete embedded
     systems from scratch — drivers to application protocols."

IF EV/CLEANTECH (ABB, H55, etc.):
  → Emphasize E2-CAD, ISO 15118, OCPP, V2G
  → Mention PLC, HomePlug Green PHY, test bench
  → "Electric mobility is a passion of mine, and my current
     V2G experience is directly applicable."

IF CONSULTING/ESN (Zuhlke, Altran, etc.):
  → Emphasize multi-sector versatility
  → Mention fast adaptation to new environments
  → "Over 20 years, I've worked in 7 different sectors,
     which allows me to adapt very quickly to any context."
