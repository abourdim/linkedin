Part 2 — Cross-Border Worker Status (Permit G)

Recommended option: cross-border worker from Haute-Savoie

You live in the Paris area. Three possible strategies:
Option A — Relocate near Geneva (cross-border worker)
  • Settle in Haute-Savoie (Annemasse, Annecy, Thonon, Gex)
  • G permit (cross-border): work in Switzerland, reside in France
  • Obligation to return home at least once per week
  • No quota for French nationals (EU/CH agreement)
  • The employer files the G permit application (2-6 weeks)
  • Taxation: withholding tax in Switzerland + tax in France (with tax credit)
  • Advantage: Swiss salary + French cost of living = optimal purchasing power
Option B — Settle directly in Switzerland
  • B permit (resident): applied for by the employer
  • More expensive (Geneva rents ~2,000 - 3,000 CHF for an apartment)
  • But no weekly return requirement
Option C — Start remotely then relocate
  • Some employers accept partial remote work
  • Start the position, then relocate after 2-3 months

G Permit Procedures

  1. Find employment (the employer must have a contract ready)
  2. The employer submits the G permit application to the canton
  3. Processing time: 2 to 6 weeks depending on the canton
  4. The G permit is valid for 5 years (permanent contract) or the duration of the contract
  5. Automatically renewable as long as you are employed

Part 3 — Adapting Your CV to the Swiss Market

Key differences in Swiss CVs

  1. PHOTO mandatory (professional, neutral background)
  2. DATE OF BIRTH to be included
  3. NATIONALITY to be mentioned
  4. MARITAL STATUS (optional but common)
  5. PERMITS: mention "Permit B" (driving licence) and "Eligible for G cross-border permit"
  6. FORMAT: 2 pages max, PDF, reverse chronological
  7. LANGUAGE: write in the language of the job posting (French for Romandie)
  8. TONE: modest, factual, precise — avoid excessive self-promotion
  9. REFERENCES: "Available upon request" is acceptable
  10. NO "CV" title at the top — start with your name

Your specific strengths for Switzerland

  • Native French + professional English + Arabic = highly valued trilingualism
  • 20+ years of experience = sought-after senior profile
  • Critical sectors (aero DO-178, medical IEC 62304) = guarantee of rigour
  • Embedded cybersecurity = rare and in-demand skill
  • Workshop-DIY association = community engagement appreciated in Switzerland

CV Action Items

  • Add a professional photo
  • Add date of birth, nationality, marital status
  • Mention "Eligible for G cross-border permit" or "B permit eligible"
  • Adapt the format to the Swiss standard (sober, professional)
  • Prepare a French version (for Romandie) and an English version (for multinationals)
  • Write a personalised cover letter for each target company
