Script

Hello, I'm Abdelhak Bourdim, a senior embedded software engineer with over 20 years of experience.

— PAUSE —

My core expertise is developing low-level embedded software — firmware, drivers, communication stacks — in C/C++ on ARM Cortex, STM32, Microchip, and NXP architectures, from bare metal to RTOS like FreeRTOS and VxWorks, and Embedded Linux.

— PAUSE —

What sets my career apart is the range of critical industries I've worked in:

In aerospace, at Safran and Thales, I developed low-level drivers and application layers compliant with DO-178.

In medical devices, at GE Healthcare and Sorin, I integrated CANOpen stacks and developed BLE modules for a pacemaker, compliant with IEC 62304.

In IoT cybersecurity, at Enedis, I led security audits and penetration tests on Linky smart meter concentrators.

And most recently, in EV charging at E2-CAD, I work on ISO 15118 integration, V2G protocols, and charging station commissioning.

— PAUSE —

Beyond the technical side, I founded Workshop-DIY, a non-profit that delivers robotics workshops for young people. It reflects my passion for sharing knowledge and innovation.

— PAUSE —

Today, I want to bring this multi-sector experience to Western Switzerland, where the quality standards and rigor match the way I work.

I'm a French national, eligible for the G cross-border permit, and available to start quickly.

I'd be happy to go into more detail about any of my projects.

Delivery Tips

  • Speak SLOWLY — Swiss pace is calmer than French pace
  • Maintain eye contact (or look at camera in video calls)
  • Factual and modest tone — no boasting
  • PAUSES are key — they show confidence
  • Do NOT recite — know the blocks, not word for word
  • If the interviewer reacts, adapt and engage in dialogue
  • Have 3 anecdotes ready to go deeper on each sector
