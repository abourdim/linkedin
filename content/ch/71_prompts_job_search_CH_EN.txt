HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS
================================================================================

1. Copy the prompt you are interested in
2. Replace the fields [IN BRACKETS] with your information
3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI
4. Adapt the response to your personal style
5. IMPORTANT: never send AI-generated text without reviewing and personalising it

RECOMMENDED ORDER:
1. Prompt 9 (analyse the posting) -> understand what the company is looking for
2. Prompt 1 or 2 (cover letter) -> apply
3. Prompt 3 or 4 (LinkedIn message) -> networking in parallel
4. Prompt 7 or 8 (interview preparation) -> before each interview
5. Prompt 10 (follow-up) -> after each interview
6. Prompt 11 (negotiation) -> when you receive an offer
