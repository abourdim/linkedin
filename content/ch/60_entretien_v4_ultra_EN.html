Hello, I am Abdelhak BOURDIM, a senior embedded software engineer.

My core expertise: developing firmware, middleware and application layers, in bare metal, under RTOS or embedded Linux, for critical and industrial systems. Over 20+ years, I have developed drivers and communication stacks — CAN, BLE, USB, TCP/IP — on ARM Cortex, STM32, Microchip and NXP.

In aerospace: at Safran on an SSPC power controller, at Thales on a multi-mode receiver N-MMR and an autonomous train system ATO, and at Zodiac on SSPCs for the A350. All in compliance with DO-178C.

In medical: at GE Healthcare on an X-ray generator with CANOpen integration, at Sorin on a Bluetooth module for pacemaker, and at PK Vitality on a glucose monitoring watch — all compliant with IEC 62304.

More recently, at Enedis I conducted a cybersecurity audit of Linky concentrators (pentest, SDR sniffing), and currently at E2-CAD I am working on electric vehicle charging stations with ISO 15118 and OCPP.

Prior to all that, 8 years at Cartadis gave me a solid foundation in complete embedded development: drivers, USB/TCP/IP stacks, RFID, encryption.

In parallel, I am the founder of Workshop-DIY, an educational robotics association where I run workshops for young people on Arduino, ESP32, AI and 3D printing.
